Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rimverse VR (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information through the Valorant VR website and game services (the “Services”).

Information We Collect

  • Account and profile data you provide or authorize through Meta, such as name, username, or profile identifier.
  • Game activity such as matches, wins, rank, scores, combat statistics, equipment use, and session duration.
  • Technical data including device type, operating system, VR platform, IP address, browser type, and diagnostic logs.
  • Support communications and optional feedback you send to us.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, deliver gameplay and matchmaking, display statistics, prevent fraud and cheating, operate customer support, analyze and improve the Services, and comply with legal obligations.

Meta Account Linking

Meta login is handled under Meta’s permissions and privacy process. We request only permissions necessary for account linking and in-game features. Your use of Meta services is also governed by Meta’s terms and policies. You may revoke application access through your Meta account settings.

Sharing Information

We may share information with service providers that operate our hosting, analytics, identity, security, and support systems. We may also share information for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or with a successor in connection with a merger or acquisition. We do not sell personal information in the ordinary meaning of that term.

Retention and Security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or required by law. We use organizational and technical safeguards, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children where local law requires age restrictions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child without legally required consent or authorization. Contact us to request deletion if we have collected such information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent or appeal a decision. We may require verification before responding.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies or local storage for authentication, preferences, security, and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling some features may affect the Services.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@valorantvr.example.
